I am an ordained Episcopal Deacon serving in the Diocese of Los Angeles.

### Current Position

The people of St. James’ (South Pasadena) have been my church community since 2014. In their midst I first discerned my call to ministry. After completing my diocesan training year at St. Luke’s/San Lucas (Long Beach), I discerned my call to the priesthood. I returned to St. James’ as a postulant in 2023 and now serve as a deacon and chaplain to the parish, coordinating pastoral care and youth ministry.

I will be ordained to the priesthood in January 2027.

**Talk to me about:** Eco-spirituality, Restorative Justice, Sacred Resistance
